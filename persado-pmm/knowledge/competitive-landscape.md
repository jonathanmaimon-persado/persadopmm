# Competitive Landscape

## Competitor types

- Traditional Agencies: Publicis, Havas, VML, McCann
- In-House Studios: internal creative teams at banks
- Generic AI Writing: Jasper, Copy.ai, ChatGPT
- Martech / Personalization: Movable Ink, Jacquard
- Optimization / Testing: Optimizely, VWO
- Compliance Software: PerformLine, Red Flag

## Where they fail
- Agencies: slow, expensive, not optimized for regulated industries
- In-House studios: capacity constrained, not specialized for compliance
- Generic AI: drafts, no compliance, no production-ready reliability
- Martech: requires manual execution and no compliance layer
- Optimization tools: do not solve content creation
- Compliance scanners: reactive, not generative
