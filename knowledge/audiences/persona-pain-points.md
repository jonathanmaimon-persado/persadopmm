# Persona Pain Points — Quick Reference

Use this for rapid targeting when writing outreach, collateral, or pitch content.

## Pain Point Matrix

| Persona | #1 Pain | #2 Pain | #3 Pain | Entry Play |
|---------|---------|---------|---------|------------|
| CMO / VP Marketing | Slow agency cycles (6-8 weeks) | High agency costs ($800K+ retainers) | Can't prove creative ROI | CREATE |
| Marketing Ops / VP Testing | Approved content underperforms | Refreshing triggers compliance delays | Media spend wasted on bad creative | OPTIMIZE |
| CRM / SFMC Owner | Template sprawl | 400 hrs/year manual refresh | Personalization limited by variant capacity | AUTOMATE |
| Compliance Officer | AI = uncontrolled risk | Generic tools have zero compliance | Review volume already overwhelming | Influencer |

## Pain-to-Message Mapping

### "Our agency cycles are too slow"
- **Lead with:** "Days, not weeks. 72-hour deployment cycle."
- **Compound with:** "At 75% lower cost than your current retainer."
- **Close with:** "And every asset is compliant before it hits your inbox."

### "We can't afford our agency anymore"
- **Lead with:** "$4-5K per production-ready asset vs. $10-15K through your agency."
- **Compound with:** "And it's 4x faster."
- **Close with:** "50 credits x $4.5K = $225K vs. 50 agency assets at $15K = $750K."

### "Our approved content is underperforming"
- **Lead with:** "Upload your top 10 underperformers. Get optimized variants in days."
- **Compound with:** "Dual-scored for performance AND compliance — no review cycle needed."
- **Close with:** "96% win rate vs. human-written and generic AI content."

### "We spend too much time on content refresh"
- **Lead with:** "87% time reduction per campaign. 345 hours saved per year."
- **Compound with:** "One-time integration. Real-time API delivers unique content per recipient."
- **Close with:** "600K personalized sends per credit."

### "AI is too risky for regulated content"
- **Lead with:** "Zero compliance incidents across all clients."
- **Compound with:** "20+ regulatory frameworks built into generation — UDAAP, TILA, Reg Z, ECOA, TCPA."
- **Close with:** "Compliance during generation, not after. Full audit trail on every asset."

### "We already use ChatGPT / Jasper"
- **Lead with:** "Great for internal drafts. But can it produce assets your compliance team will approve?"
- **Compound with:** "Persado's 96% win rate is against both human-written AND generic LLM content."
- **Close with:** "Production-ready, not drafts. 20+ regulatory frameworks. Zero incidents."

## Discovery Questions by Persona

### For CMOs / VPs Marketing
1. "How long does it typically take from brief to live campaign?"
2. "What percentage of your creative budget goes to agency retainers?"
3. "How many campaigns did you have to delay or cut last year due to production bottlenecks?"
4. "If you could launch campaigns in days instead of weeks, what would you do differently?"

### For Marketing Ops / Testing Leaders
1. "How many of your current live assets would you say are underperforming?"
2. "What happens when you want to refresh an approved asset — how long is the compliance review?"
3. "How many variants do you test per campaign? How many would you test if production wasn't a bottleneck?"
4. "What's the impact on media spend when creative underperforms?"

### For CRM / SFMC Owners
1. "How many email templates are you currently managing?"
2. "How many hours per week does your team spend on content refresh?"
3. "What does your personalization strategy look like today — dynamic fields, or something more?"
4. "If content production were automated, what would your team focus on instead?"

### For Compliance Officers
1. "How do you currently review AI-generated content before it goes to market?"
2. "Which regulatory frameworks do you find most challenging for marketing content?"
3. "What's your biggest concern about AI in customer-facing communications?"
4. "If content arrived pre-validated against your regulatory requirements with a full audit trail, how would that change your review process?"
