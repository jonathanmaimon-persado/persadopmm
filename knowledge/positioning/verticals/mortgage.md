# Mortgage / Lending

## Vertical Profile

**Primary pain:** Rate windows close before campaigns can launch. Seasonal timing + compliance = missed market windows. When rates drop, every lender scrambles — but the ones still waiting on agency cycles lose the refinance wave.

**Secondary pains:**
- Spring homebuying season campaigns need to launch in Q1 — but production starts in Q4 of the prior year
- Refinance windows are measured in weeks, not months
- Mortgage compliance is among the most stringent in financial services
- State-level regulations add complexity on top of federal requirements
- Rate change communications require near-real-time content updates

## Key Regulations

- **RESPA** (Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act) — disclosure requirements, anti-kickback provisions
- **TILA** (Truth in Lending Act) — APR disclosures, rate lock terms, fee transparency
- **ECOA** (Equal Credit Opportunity Act) — non-discriminatory lending language
- **Fair Lending** — prohibits discriminatory marketing practices
- **State-level regulations** — vary by state; add complexity to multi-state campaigns
- **HMDA-adjacent** — messaging must not imply selective lending

## The Rate Window Problem

This is the most time-sensitive vertical. When mortgage rates shift:

1. **Day 0:** Rate change announced
2. **Day 1-2:** Strategy and brief developed
3. **Weeks 3-8 (agency model):** Creative production, compliance review, rework
4. **Week 9+:** Campaign launches — but the rate window may have already closed
