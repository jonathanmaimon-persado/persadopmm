# Product Streams

## CREATE
- Target: CMO / creative budget owners
- Outcome: brief to production-ready asset in days
- Benefit: 75% lower cost, compliance built in

## OPTIMIZE
- Target: marketing ops / testing leaders
- Outcome: underperforming assets refreshed safely
- Benefit: performance + compliance scoring, fewer rejections

## AUTOMATE
- Target: CRM / SFMC owners
- Outcome: one-time integration for real-time content delivery
- Benefit: 87% time reduction, fewer manual campaigns
