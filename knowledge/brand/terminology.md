# Terminology Guide

## Persado-Specific Terms

| Term | Definition | Usage Notes |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| CREATE | Accelerated production play — brief to production-ready asset | Always capitalize when referring to the play |
| OPTIMIZE | Safe performance refresh play — upload, score, generate variants | Always capitalize when referring to the play |
| AUTOMATE | Real-time personalization play — API-driven content delivery | Always capitalize when referring to the play |
| The Three Plays | CREATE, OPTIMIZE, AUTOMATE as a group | Use when referencing the full solution set |
| Performance Prediction | Scored 0-100 based on 1M+ A/B tests | Platform capability, not a marketing claim |
| Compliance Scoring | Real-time analysis against regulatory frameworks | Use "during generation, not after" framing |
| Variant Rationale | Explainable AI — why each word was chosen | Differentiator vs. black-box AI tools |
| Creative Analysis Report | Full audit trail for a generated asset | Part of the compliance story |

## Competitor References

| Use This | Not This | Why |
|----------|----------|-----|
| Traditional agencies | Creative agencies | "Traditional" implies outdated |
| Agency retainers | Agency fees | "Retainers" sounds more expensive and locked-in |
| Generic AI tools | AI competitors | Frames them as generic, not specialized |
| Drafts (their output) | Content (their output) | "Drafts" signals incomplete; "content" signals finished |
| Production-ready (our output) | Content (our output) | "Production-ready" is the differentiator |

## Phrases to Standardize

| Standard Phrase | Avoid |
|----------------|-------|
| Compliance during generation, not after | Built-in compliance, compliance-first |
| Production-ready | Market-ready, campaign-ready |
| Days, not weeks | Faster, quickly, rapidly |
| Brief to production-ready | End-to-end, full-service |
| 20+ regulatory frameworks | Multiple regulations, various compliance standards |
| Purpose-built for regulated industries | Designed for financial services, built for banks |
| Dissolves the trade-off | Balances speed and compliance, manages the tension |

## Capitalization Rules

- **CREATE, OPTIMIZE, AUTOMATE** — all caps when referring to the plays
- **Persado** — always capitalized, never "PERSADO" or "persado"
- **Financial services** — lowercase unless starting a sentence
- Regulatory acronyms — always uppercase (UDAAP, TILA, ECOA)
- Job titles — capitalize when used as proper titles before names, lowercase otherwise
