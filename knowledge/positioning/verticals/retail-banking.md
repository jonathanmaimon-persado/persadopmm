# Retail Banking / Credit Cards (Issuer-Led)

## Vertical Profile

**Primary pain:** Acquisition volume constrained by content production bottleneck. Card offers sit in compliance review while competitors launch. Seasonal campaigns miss their windows.

**Secondary pains:**
- Agency retainers consuming budget that could go to media spend
- Compliance rework cycles adding 2-4 weeks per campaign
- Testing velocity limited by variant production capacity
- Rate-change communications need rapid turnaround

## Speed Advantage

Launch card offers in days, not months.

**Specific hooks:**
- "Your Q2 balance transfer campaign — live before your competitors brief their agencies."
- "Card acquisition emails in 72 hours, not 6 weeks."
- "Rate change? New offer live by end of week."

## Key Regulations

- **TILA** (Truth in Lending Act) — APR disclosures, fee disclosures, promotional rate terms
- **UDAAP** (Unfair, Deceptive, or Abusive Acts or Practices) — fair marketing language, no misleading claims
- **ECOA** (Equal Credit Opportunity Act) — non-discriminatory language in offers
- **TCPA** (Telephone Consumer Protection Act) — consent and opt-out compliance for digital communications

## Relevant Proof Points

- 8/10 largest U.S. banks in production
- +45% click lift / +36% conversion lift — Top 10 U.S. bank, cards campaign
- $1B+ incremental value at one bank (cards & mortgage, over 4 years)
- 96% win rate vs. human and generic AI content

## Consumer Banking (Deposits / Checking / Savings)

**Primary pain:** High frequency of communications, trust/clarity required, rate changes require fast reactions. Every basis point change requires updated content across channels.

**Speed advantage:** Variants at scale, fast reaction to rate shifts.

**Regulations:** UDAAP, ECOA, state-level

**Specific hooks:**
- "Rate change on Tuesday. Updated content across all channels by Thursday."
- "Savings rate promotions that are compliant before your compliance team reviews them."

**Campaign types:**
- Savings rate promotions
- Checking account acquisition
- CD maturity / renewal
- Digital banking adoption
- Branch experience communications
- Fee structure changes
