# Fintech

## Vertical Profile

**Primary pain:** Rapid growth environments need acquisition + onboarding velocity. Fintechs move fast but often lack mature compliance infrastructure — which creates risk as they scale into regulated products (lending, banking, insurance).

**Secondary pains:**
- Scaling content production without scaling headcount
- Moving from "startup speed" to "regulated speed" as products mature
- Building compliance processes from scratch is expensive and slow
- Competing with incumbents who have decades of brand trust
- Need to prove credibility to regulators while maintaining growth velocity

## Key Regulations

Varies by product and charter status — often less mature compliance infrastructure, which makes built-in compliance more valuable.

- **Lending fintechs:** TILA, ECOA, Fair Lending, state licensing requirements
- **Neobanks / banking fintechs:** UDAAP, ECOA, partner bank requirements
- **Insurance fintechs:** State insurance regulations, disclosure requirements
- **Payment fintechs:** EFTA, Reg E, state money transmitter rules
- **BNPL:** Emerging regulatory frameworks, CFPB guidance

## The Compliance Maturity Gap

This is Persado's unique wedge in fintech. Most fintechs:

1. **Early stage:** Move fast, minimal compliance (pre-regulatory scrutiny)
2. **Growth stage:** Regulators start watching, need to build compliance processes
3. **Scale stage:** Full regulatory compliance required, often retroactively

Persado lets fintechs skip the painful (and expensive) middle phase by building compliance into content generation from day one. Instead of hiring a compliance team to review every campaign, compliance is automatic.
