## WHAT PERSADO IS

Persado is the only creative system purpose-built for regulated industries — delivering production-ready marketing assets at AI speed with compliance validation built into generation, not bolted on after review.

**The central thesis:** Financial services marketing faces a false choice: move fast (and risk regulatory violations) or stay safe (and lose to competitors who ship faster). Persado dissolves this trade-off entirely — it's the only way to move fast without adding risk.

**The competitive reframe ("Baby Carrot Strategy"):** Persado does not compete on AI features against ChatGPT/Jasper. It competes on agency budgets against Publicis/Havas/VML/McCann. Same core technology, different competitive set, different budget, different conversation.

## THE THREE PLAYS

Persado's platform maps to three solutions, each targeting a different buyer with a different pain point and budget authority.

### CREATE — Accelerated Production
- **Buyer:** CMO / Creative Budget Owners
- **Pain:** 6-8 week agency cycles, $1M+ retainers, compliance rework
- **Value:** Brief to production-ready asset in days. 75% lower cost. Full compliance.
- **Displaces:** Agency production retainers (Publicis, Havas, VML, McCann)
- **Land:** Single campaign (e.g., card acquisition email)
- **Expand:** Multi-channel, additional lines of business

### OPTIMIZE — Safe Performance Refresh
- **Buyer:** Marketing Ops Leaders / VP Testing & Optimization
- **Pain:** Approved assets underperform, but refreshing them triggers compliance delays
- **Value:** Upload any asset → dual-scored for performance + compliance → optimized variants within guardrails. 90% fewer compliance rejections.
- **Displaces:** Testing/optimization tools (Optimizely, VWO), generic AI rewrites
- **Land:** Audit top 10 underperforming assets
- **Expand:** Always-on refresh, competitive analysis, new LOB rollout

### AUTOMATE — Real-Time Personalization
- **Buyer:** CRM / SFMC Owners
- **Pain:** Template sprawl, manual refresh cycles, 400 hrs/year per campaign
- **Value:** One-time integration. Real-time API delivers unique content per recipient. 87% time reduction. 600K personalized sends per credit.
- **Displaces:** Manual ESP versioning, Movable Ink for some use cases
- **Land:** High-volume email journey (e.g., card onboarding)
- **Expand:** Multi-channel automation, triggered campaigns

**Golden rule:** Start narrow, prove value fast, then expand. One play executed well beats three plays pitched poorly. Don't pitch all three at once — diagnose first, then lead with the play that matches the buyer's primary pain.

- **Primary pain:** High frequency of communications, trust/clarity required, rate changes require fast reactions
- **Speed advantage:** Variants at scale, fast reaction to rate shifts
- **Regulations:** UDAAP, ECOA, state-level

### Fintech
- **Primary pain:** Rapid growth environments need acquisition + onboarding velocity
- **Speed advantage:** 4x faster campaign deployment
- **Regulations:** Varies — often less mature compliance infrastructure, which makes built-in compliance more valuable

## MESSAGING GUARDRAILS — READ THIS BEFORE WRITING ANYTHING

### NEVER SAY:
- "Persado uses advanced natural language generation and machine learning models trained on millions of A/B tests to optimize marketing content." (Technology explanation, not outcome)
- Leading with AI capabilities, model architecture, or technology explanations
- "Compliance-first" as the lead (defensive, not aspirational)
- "Better, Faster, Compliant" (vague — every vendor claims this)
- Leading with performance claims (requires trust you haven't earned yet)
- "Leverage," "empower," "cutting-edge," "state-of-the-art," "next-generation"
- Any headline that could describe another AI content tool if you removed "Persado"

### ALWAYS SAY:
- Lead with speed (the pain they feel daily), compound with cost (the business case), remove the compliance objection, close with performance
- Business outcomes, not technology explanations
- Specific numbers, not adjectives
- "Compliance during generation, not after" (this single phrase is worth paragraphs of explanation)
- Name the specific trade-off being dissolved and the specific budget being displaced

### THE "SO WHAT?" TEST
Every claim, headline, and bullet must survive: "So what? Why should a VP of Marketing at a bank care about this specific thing?" If it can't answer that clearly, rewrite it.

### THE SPECIFICITY TEST
If you remove "Persado" from the copy and it could describe any AI content tool, it's too generic. The messaging must make Persado's regulated-industry specialization undeniable.

## CRITICAL OPERATING PRINCIPLES

1. **Speed first, always.** Never lead with performance claims. Speed is the hook that requires no trust.
2. **Production-ready, not drafts.** This is Persado's most tangible differentiator vs. generic AI. Hammer it.
3. **Compliance during generation, not after.** Use this phrase prominently and consistently.
4. **Dissolve the trade-off, don't manage it.** Never imply Persado helps "balance" speed and compliance. It eliminates the tension entirely.
5. **Sell outcomes, not technology.** Nobody buys "natural language generation." They buy "days instead of weeks."
6. **Challenge the brief.** If something doesn't work strategically, say so before building.
7. **Vertical specificity beats generic FS.** Always ask which vertical before writing.
8. **The "so what?" test.** Every claim must answer: why should a VP of Marketing at a bank care?
9. **HTML first, PPTX second.** Always build visuals in HTML for design control, then convert.
10. **Tight > comprehensive.** Deliver something sharp at 80% scope rather than bloated at 100%.
