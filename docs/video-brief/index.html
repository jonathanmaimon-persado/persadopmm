Concept 01 · Category-defining

The Content Supply Chain

System of Reference → Engagement → Intelligence
Thesis

The bottleneck is not creative. It's the supply chain that produces, validates, and ships content.

Regulated industries are trying to solve this with a repository of approved assets — reuse or remix. That solves maybe 10% of the problem. Every new combination still needs review, and when the Fed changes rates, you're rummaging through the vault while a competitor is in market with a campaign designed for the new world.

Persado is the missing System of Intelligence: a layer that sits between your System of Reference (compliance- approved language, brand kit, regulatory frameworks) and your System of Engagement (CRM, MAP, ad platforms), turning a finite library into infinite, scored, born-compliant variants.

Concept 02 · Technological depth

Predictions & the Intelligence Layer

Every variant scored before it ships.
Thesis

You shouldn't have to send a campaign to find out it doesn't work.

Most marketing teams ship variants and wait. Generic AI tools accelerate the drafting, but they still send blind — they have no way to know which variant will win. Persado's intelligence layer turns the question around: every generated variant comes back with a predicted lift, a confidence interval, and an explanation of why.

The substrate: 1T+ messages analyzed, 120K+ campaigns labeled with outcomes, 100K+ FS-specific pre-validated message elements. The result is a topology of behavioral language — peaks and valleys of performance — that lets us locate any new variant on the map before it leaves the building.

Concept 03 · Moat & client value

Brand Fit → Client-Specific Models

Sounds like you. Compounds every cycle.
Thesis

Generic AI sounds like everyone. Persado learns to sound like only you — and gets sharper every campaign.

The hidden cost of generic LLMs in regulated marketing isn't accuracy; it's homogeneity. Drop the same prompt into the same model at three banks and the headlines could be swapped without anyone noticing. Brand becomes decorative — a logo bolted on top of vanilla copy.

Persado starts with a brand kit — voice, lexicon, do/don't examples, tone vectors — and uses it as the boundary inside which it generates. Then every campaign runs as a designed experiment. Winning variants amplify the fingerprint. Losing variants decay it. After ten cycles, the model isn't "an FS model" anymore. It's yours. A competitor adopting the same generic LLM gets the same vanilla starting point you abandoned three quarters ago.

MOF differentiation · objection handling ("why not just use ChatGPT?")

Concept 04 · Trust & risk removal

Compliance to Certainty

Built into generation, not after.
Thesis

Compliance is the bottleneck. Persado moves it from a downstream gate into a property of generation itself.

Every generic-AI workflow in regulated marketing hits the same wall: drafts come back fast, then 60–90% get rejected or revised by compliance. The "10/90 Rule" — generic AI accelerates the 10% that was already easy, and leaves the 90% (the review queue) untouched.

Persado's compliance-during-generation model bakes 20+ regulatory frameworks and 100K+ FS-pre-validated message elements into the generation step. Variants come out the other side already labeled compliant. Result: 90% fewer compliance rejections, zero compliance incidents in 10+ years of operation, 72-hour brief-to-deploy.

Persado.com is a light-mode-dominant site (warm off-white canvas, Persado red as accent, product-illustration cards). Site-native · LIGHT

4 hero loops. Warm #FAFAF7 canvas · dark ink · Persado red as solid accent (no glow). Asset cards on white.

Why: lives on persado.com.

Product-heavy · WARM PAPER

4 product-track social spots (Create + Optimize launch). Marketing canvas #FAF8F4 + product UI cards inside (briefs, score panels, compliance checks, deployed assets). Mona Sans. Two-palette system.

Why: extends the existing benchmark loops; supports Create public launch. See the design-prompts companion file.

Feed-native / cinematic · DARK

4 general social spots + 4 product videos + 4 walkthroughs. Canvas #0B0B14 · white ink · red glow halos · neon score chips.

Product-heavy register raises fidelity further by adding the Workfront-ticket pattern, status logs, brand guardrails ribbon, approval bars, and audit-trail panels developed in the Claude Design benchmark.

Motion principles

01
Process is visible, never decorative.

Every animation conveys a beat of the argument — a layer revealing, a variant scoring, a lane filling. If a motion doesn't carry meaning, remove it.

02
No bounce. No spin. No physics drama.

Easing is cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1) for entrances, cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1) for transitions. Drawing is left-to-right or center-out. Loaders should not exist — if a frame would need one, redesign.

03
Numbers count. Type does not.

Headlines and labels fade in or hold; counters use a clean count-up with cubic ease. The exception is the social spots, where each hook line types in for thumb-stop attention.

04
No Persado UI. But real customer assets.

Never show a Persado dashboard, panel, or workflow screen — the product is the intelligence, not the buttons. Do show the assets the customer ships: a rendered email, a paid-social card, an SMS bubble, a homepage hero. These are the customer outcomes; they make the abstract metaphors land. Walkthroughs use a fictional client identity (FNC Credit, navy #1E3A8A) for safe-to-show mocks; swap to a named client in pre-pitch versions if cleared.

05
Sound is optional. Silence is the default.

Walkthroughs get an ambient pad (A minor, low-pulse synth) + VO. Social spots ship caption-first, sound-off. Hero loops have no audio.

06
Specificity over abstraction in copy.

"Approved-content repository" beats "old way." "FED CUTS RATES · 25 BPS" beats "market moves." Specifics make the metaphor land.
