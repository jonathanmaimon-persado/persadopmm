# Approved Headlines

All headlines below have been vetted against the messaging hierarchy, guardrails, "so what?" test, and specificity test. Use and adapt freely.

## Speed-Led Headlines (Use as Primary Hook)

### Generic Financial Services
- "Days, not weeks. Production-ready marketing at AI speed."
- "Your agency takes 6 weeks. Persado takes 72 hours."
- "Brief to production-ready. No agency timeline. No compliance rework."
- "What your agency delivers in 6 weeks, Persado delivers in days."
- "The 6-week campaign cycle is over."

### Credit Cards
- "Launch card offers in days, not months."
- "Your Q2 balance transfer campaign — live before your competitors brief their agencies."
- "Card acquisition emails in 72 hours. Not 6 weeks."

### Mortgage
- "Rates just dropped. Your refinance campaign is live by Friday."
- "Spring homebuying content — ready in January, not March."
- "Rate windows don't wait for agency timelines."

### Co-Brand
- "One brief. Both brands. Production-ready in 72 hours."
- "Dual-brand compliance in the generation, not in the review cycle."

### Fintech
- "Grow at startup speed. Comply at bank-grade standards."
- "4x more campaigns. Same team. Full compliance."

## Cost-Led Headlines (Use as Secondary Hook)

- "50 production-ready assets for the cost of 15 from your agency."
- "Redirect $525K from agency retainers to media spend."
- "$4-5K per asset vs. $10-15K through your agency."
- "Same quality. 75% less cost. No 6-week wait."
- "The $750K question: why are you still paying agency rates for content?"

## Compliance Headlines (Use to Remove the Objection)

- "Compliance during generation, not after."
- "20+ regulatory frameworks. Zero incidents. Every time."
- "The only way to move fast without adding risk."
- "Fast and compliant aren't trade-offs. They're multipliers."
- "Zero compliance incidents isn't a goal. It's our track record."

## Performance Headlines (Use to Close)

- "96% win rate vs. human-written and generic AI content."
- "Faster. Cheaper. Compliant. And it performs better."
- "+43% conversion lift. A/B tested. Not a promise — a measurement."
- "$1B+ in incremental value at a single Top 10 bank."

## Compound Headlines (Speed + Cost or Speed + Compliance)

- "Days instead of weeks. 75% less than your agency. Fully compliant."
- "4x faster. 75% cheaper. Zero compliance incidents."
- "The only creative system that moves at AI speed with bank-grade compliance."
- "Production-ready in 72 hours. Compliant across 20+ frameworks. No agency required."

## Headlines to NEVER Use (Common Mistakes)

- "AI-powered content creation for financial services" (generic — any AI tool)
- "Better marketing with artificial intelligence" (vague — fails specificity test)
- "Unlock the power of AI for your campaigns" (banned phrase + generic)
- "Compliant AI content generation" (compliance-first = defensive)
- "Next-generation marketing technology" (banned phrase + no substance)
