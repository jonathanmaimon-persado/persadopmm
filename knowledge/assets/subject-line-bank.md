# Subject Line Bank — Outreach Campaigns

All subject lines follow the messaging hierarchy: speed hook first, cost compounds, compliance removes objection.

## Rules for Subject Lines

1. Under 50 characters (ideally under 40 for mobile)
2. No banned words (leverage, empower, cutting-edge, etc.)
3. Must pass the "so what?" test
4. Must pass the specificity test (not generic AI)
5. Personalization fields in {{brackets}}

## Speed-Hook Subject Lines

### Direct / Provocative
- "6 weeks is too long"
- "Your agency is too slow"
- "{{Company}} campaigns in days, not weeks"
- "72 hours from brief to live"
- "What takes 6 weeks today"
- "Your competitors ship faster"

### Question-Based
- "How long is your campaign cycle?"
- "Still waiting on your agency?"
- "What if campaigns launched in days?"
- "{{FirstName}}, how fast is too fast?"

### Vertical-Specific (Cards)
- "Card offers in days, not months"
- "Your Q2 campaign — ready now"
- "{{Company}} card acquisition, faster"

### Vertical-Specific (Mortgage)
- "Rates dropped. Are you ready?"
- "Spring campaigns ready in January"
- "Refi campaigns in 72 hours"

### Vertical-Specific (Co-Brand)
- "Dual approval in half the time"
- "One brief, both brands"

## Cost-Hook Subject Lines

- "$525K back in your budget"
- "75% less than your agency"
- "The $750K question"
- "50 assets for the cost of 15"
- "$4K vs $15K per asset"
- "Your agency retainer, reimagined"

## Compliance-Hook Subject Lines

(Use sparingly — compliance is better as the response to the speed hook, not the opener)

- "Fast AND compliant. Not a trade-off."
- "Zero compliance incidents"
- "Compliant before review"
- "20 regulatory frameworks, built in"

## Follow-Up Subject Lines

### After No Response (Sequence Email 2-3)
- "Quick thought on {{Company}} campaigns"
- "One number worth seeing"
- "Bumping this — 72 hours"
- "Not sure if you saw this"

### After Engagement (Opened / Clicked)
- "The {{Company}} case for faster campaigns"
- "Here's how {{SimilarCompany}} did it"
- "A quick look at the numbers"
- "Worth 15 minutes, {{FirstName}}?"

### Breakup / Final Touch
- "Last note on this"
- "Closing the loop"
- "One last thought"

## Subject Line A/B Testing Notes

- Always test at least 2 subject lines per campaign
- Speed-hook subject lines consistently outperform cost-hook subject lines in cold outreach
- Question-based subject lines perform well for first touch
- Specific numbers in subject lines (72 hours, 75%, $525K) outperform vague claims
- Personalization ({{FirstName}} or {{Company}}) adds 15-25% open rate lift
