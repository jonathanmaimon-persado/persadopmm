## Usage Guidelines

1. **Always provide context** — raw numbers without framing are less persuasive
2. **Match stats to audience** — CMOs care about speed/cost; Compliance officers care about zero incidents; CRM owners care about time savings
3. **Use $2.5B carefully** — needs methodology context for CFO audiences
4. **Performance stats come last** — per the messaging hierarchy, only after speed, cost, and compliance have established credibility
5. **Pair stats with the specific pain they solve** — "72-hour deployment cycle" paired with "versus 6-8 week agency timelines"
