# Messaging Hierarchy

This is the single most important document in the repository. Do not deviate from this order.

## The Order (Non-Negotiable)

### 1. SPEED — The Hook

**Message:** 4x faster — days, not weeks

**Role:** Gets attention. Requires no trust. Every marketer in financial services knows the pain of 6-8 week production cycles. This is the universal entry point.

**How to use it:**
- Always lead with speed in headlines, subject lines, and opening statements
- Anchor to the specific pain: "6-8 week agency cycles" becomes "days, not weeks"
- Use the 72-hour deployment cycle stat for credibility
- Speed is felt, not argued — it resonates immediately

**Example framings:**
- "What takes your agency 6 weeks, Persado delivers in days."
- "Your spring mortgage campaign — ready in January, not March."
- "72 hours from brief to production-ready asset."

### 2. COST — Compounds the Value

**Message:** 75% lower production costs / agency displacement

**Role:** Turns the speed hook into a business case. Speed already implies savings; making it explicit with numbers compounds the value. This is verifiable — they know what they pay agencies.

**How to use it:**
- Always pair with specific budget displacement: "$15K per agency asset vs. $4-5K through Persado"
- Reference agency retainer totals: "$750K agency spend becomes $225K"
- Position as budget reallocation, not just cost cutting
- Use the "Baby Carrot Strategy" — compete on agency budgets, not AI feature budgets

**Example framings:**
- "50 production-ready assets for the cost of 15 from your agency."
- "Redirect $525K from agency retainers to media spend."
- "Same quality. 75% less cost. No 6-week wait."

### 3. COMPLIANCE — Removes the Objection

**Message:** 100% compliant, zero incidents

**Role:** The moment you say "fast and cheap," every regulated-industry buyer thinks "but what about compliance?" This answers the unspoken objection before it becomes a blocker.

**How to use it:**
- Position as the natural next question: "And yes, it's fully compliant"
- Use the key phrase: "Compliance during generation, not after"
- Reference 20+ regulatory frameworks built into generation
- Emphasize zero compliance incidents across all clients
- Compliance is fused with speed — they are multipliers, not trade-offs

**Example framings:**
- "Compliance during generation, not after — 20+ regulatory frameworks built in."
- "Zero compliance incidents. Not because we're careful. Because it's automatic."
- "The only way to move fast without adding risk."

### 4. PERFORMANCE — Closes the Deal

**Message:** 96% win rate, 20-40% conversion lift

**Role:** Proves that faster + cheaper + compliant does not equal worse. This requires trust, so it comes AFTER speed, cost, and compliance credibility are established.

**How to use it:**
- Only introduce after the first three messages have landed
- Use in deeper conversations, case studies, and proof-of-concept proposals
- Reference specific results: "+45% click lift at a Top 10 U.S. bank"
- Position as the "and also" — the bonus on top of speed, cost, and compliance

**Example framings:**
- "And the content doesn't just ship faster — it performs better. 96% win rate vs. human-written and generic AI."
- "+43% average conversion lift, A/B tested across cards, mortgage, deposits, insurance."
- "$1B+ in incremental value generated at a single Top 10 bank."

## Critical Nuance

Speed and compliance are fused and inseparable. The messaging must present them as multipliers of each other, not as separate concerns.

**The positioning that works:** "The only way to move fast without adding risk."

**NOT:** "Compliant and also fast."

The hierarchy is about conversational sequencing — which message opens the door, which builds the case, which removes doubt, which closes. It is NOT about relative importance. All four matter. The order is about buyer psychology.
