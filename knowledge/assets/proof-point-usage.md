# Proof Point Usage Guide

How to deploy proof points effectively in different contexts.

## Proof Points by Messaging Stage

### Stage 1: Speed (The Hook)
| Proof Point | Best Used In | Example Framing |
|-------------|-------------|-----------------|
| 72-hour deployment cycle | Outreach, headlines, pitch openers | "Brief to production-ready in 72 hours." |
| 4x faster | Comparison slides, battle cards | "What takes your agency 6 weeks, Persado delivers in days." |
| 87% time reduction | AUTOMATE pitch | "87% less time per campaign — 345 hours saved per year." |

### Stage 2: Cost (Compounds Value)
| Proof Point | Best Used In | Example Framing |
|-------------|-------------|-----------------|
| 75% lower cost | Business case slides, ROI calculators | "75% lower production costs than your current agency retainer." |
| $4-5K per asset vs. $10-15K | Cost comparison slides, one-pagers | "Production-ready assets at $4-5K vs. $10-15K through your agency." |
| $525K savings math | Executive presentations, CFO-facing content | "50 assets: $225K with Persado vs. $750K with your agency." |

### Stage 3: Compliance (Removes Objection)
| Proof Point | Best Used In | Example Framing |
|-------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Zero compliance incidents | Compliance officer conversations, risk sections | "Zero compliance incidents across all clients. Full audit trail." |
| 90% fewer compliance rejections | Process improvement slides | "90% fewer compliance rejections — because compliance is built into generation." |
| 20+ regulatory frameworks | Detail slides, compliance deep-dives | "UDAAP, TILA, Reg Z, ECOA, TCPA, RESPA, Fair Lending, state-level, plus custom." |

### Stage 4: Performance (Closes Deal)
| Proof Point | Best Used In | Example Framing |
|-------------|-------------|-----------------|
| 96% win rate | Case studies, proof-of-concept proposals | "96% win rate vs. human-written AND generic LLM content." |
| +43% conversion lift | Results slides, executive summaries | "+43% average conversion lift across cards, mortgage, deposits, insurance." |
| $1B+ at one bank | Enterprise logos slide, credibility section | "$1B+ incremental value at a single Top 10 U.S. bank." |
| +45% click / +36% conversion | Card-specific case studies | "+45% click lift and +36% conversion lift for a Top 10 bank's cards campaign." |

## Proof Points by Audience

### For CMOs
Lead with: 72-hour cycle, 75% cost reduction, $525K savings math
Support with: 8/10 largest banks, 96% win rate
Avoid leading with: Technical stats, compliance details

### For Marketing Ops
Lead with: 96% win rate, +43% conversion lift, 90% fewer rejections
Support with: 1M+ A/B tests, 120K+ campaigns
Avoid leading with: Cost stats (not their budget)

### For CRM / SFMC Owners
Lead with: 87% time reduction, 345 hours saved, 600K sends per credit
Support with: 400 hrs/year pain point (their current state)
Avoid leading with: Agency cost comparisons (not their problem)

### For Compliance Officers
Lead with: Zero incidents, 20+ frameworks, 90% fewer rejections
Support with: Audit trail, specific framework names
Avoid leading with: Speed or cost stats (sounds like cutting corners)

## Proof Points by Deliverable Type

### Outreach Emails
- Use 1-2 proof points maximum per email
- Lead with speed stat, support with one cost or compliance stat
- Never stack more than 3 numbers in a single email

### Pitch Decks
- One hero stat per slide
- Build proof points progressively through the deck following the hierarchy
- Use comparison formats: before/after, us/them

### One-Pagers
- Hero stat at top (largest font)
- 3-4 supporting stats in the body
- Match stats to the specific play (CREATE/OPTIMIZE/AUTOMATE)

### Battle Cards
- Head-to-head comparison stats
- Focus on where competitors fail and Persado's corresponding proof
- Include 2-3 "counter-stats" for common competitor claims

## The $2.5B Stat — Special Handling

**Stat:** $2.5B+ incremental value generated

**What it means:** Measured incremental revenue (Persado vs. control) across all communications, over 4 years.

**When to use:** Enterprise presentations, credibility sections, executive-level conversations.

**When NOT to use:** Cold outreach (too big to be credible without context), compliance-focused conversations (irrelevant).

**Required context:** Always explain the methodology — "measured as incremental revenue, Persado-generated content versus control, across all client communications, over four years." Without context, CFOs will dismiss it.
