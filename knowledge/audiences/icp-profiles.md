# Ideal Customer Profiles (ICP)

## Primary Buyers

### CMO / VP Marketing / Creative Budget Owners

**Profile:**
- Controls creative production budget ($1M+ in agency retainers at enterprise scale)
- Measured on campaign velocity, cost efficiency, and market responsiveness
- Frustrated by agency timelines that make them look slow
- Under pressure from CEO/board to "use AI" but unsure how without compliance risk

**Pain points:**
- Agency costs consuming budget that could go to media spend or new initiatives
- 6-8 week production cycles making them uncompetitive
- Can't prove ROI on creative spend — agency output is a black box
- Board asks "why aren't you using AI?" but compliance team blocks generic tools

**What they care about:**
1. Speed to market (how fast can I launch?)
2. Cost reduction (how much can I save?)
3. Competitive advantage (am I faster than peers?)
4. Risk mitigation (will this create compliance problems?)

**Entry play:** CREATE

**Conversation opener:** Speed and cost. "What if you could get 50 production-ready assets for the cost of 15 from your agency — in days, not weeks?"

**Budget they control:** Agency production retainers, creative services spend, campaign production budgets

### Marketing Ops Leaders / VP Testing & Optimization

**Profile:**
- Owns the testing and optimization stack
- Measured on conversion rates, test velocity, and media efficiency
- Has approved assets that underperform but can't refresh them without compliance delays
- Knows the content is the problem but doesn't control content production

**Pain points:**
- Approved assets underperform, but refreshing triggers 2-4 week compliance review
- Media spend wasted on suboptimal creative (better creative = better ROAS)
- Testing velocity limited by how fast variants can be produced
- Optimization tools (Optimizely, VWO) test variants but don't create them

**What they care about:**
1. Performance lift without compliance risk
2. Testing velocity (more variants, faster)
3. Media spend efficiency
4. Quantifiable results (before/after metrics)

**Entry play:** OPTIMIZE

**Conversation opener:** "What if you could take your top 10 underperforming assets, score them for both performance and compliance, and get optimized variants — without triggering a compliance review cycle?"

**Budget they control:** Testing/experimentation tools, optimization platforms

---

### CRM / SFMC Owners

**Profile:**
- Manages email and CRM communication infrastructure
- Measured on engagement metrics, operational efficiency, and personalization quality
- Drowning in template management and manual refresh cycles
- Spends 400+ hours per year per campaign on content refresh

**Pain points:**
- Template sprawl — hundreds of templates with marginal variations
- Manual refresh cycles consuming team bandwidth
- Personalization limited by variant production capacity
- 400 hrs/year per campaign on content operations

**What they care about:**
1. Automation and time savings
2. Personalization at scale
3. Operational efficiency
4. Reducing manual content operations

**Entry play:** AUTOMATE

**Conversation opener:** "How many hours does your team spend per year refreshing email content? What if that dropped by 87%?"

**Budget they control:** Personalization technology spend, ESP/CRM tooling

## Influencer (Not Buyer)

### Compliance Officers / Chief Compliance Officers

**Profile:**
- Does not control budget but can block or accelerate any deal
- Measured on zero regulatory incidents and audit readiness
- Deeply skeptical of AI in customer-facing content
- Has seen generic AI tools produce non-compliant content

**Pain points:**
- AI creates uncontrolled regulatory risk
- Generic tools (ChatGPT, Jasper) have zero compliance awareness
- Every new marketing tool means more content to review
- Already overwhelmed with review volume

**What they care about:**
1. Audit trails and documentation
2. Regulatory framework coverage (specific frameworks, not vague "compliance")
3. Zero incidents track record
4. Control and visibility over what goes to market

**Role in deal:** Can block or accelerate. Persado's goal is to turn compliance from blocker to champion.

**How to engage them:**
- Lead with zero compliance incidents across all clients
- Reference specific frameworks by name (UDAAP, TILA, Reg Z, ECOA, TCPA, RESPA)
- Emphasize "compliance during generation, not after" — reduces their review burden
- Show the audit trail and Creative Analysis Report
- Position as: "This actually makes your job easier. Less to review, fewer rejections, full documentation."
