## THE 400-HOUR STORY (AUTOMATE-SPECIFIC)

This is a self-contained proof narrative for the AUTOMATE play. Use it as a complete story arc.

## STAT CONFLICTS AND RESOLUTION

Some stats appear with slight variations across different Persado source materials. Here's how to resolve conflicts:

| Stat Area | Variant A | Variant B | Resolution |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| Win rate | 96% | 95% | Use 96% for A/B test precision, 95% for broader claims. Never both in same doc. |
| Regulatory frameworks | 20+ | 50+ | 20+ appears in most materials and is conservative/defensible. 50+ appears in the CREATE Pitch Deck. Use 20+ as default; 50+ only in contexts where the CREATE deck is the source. |
| Incremental value | $2.5B | $2.5B+ | Always use $2.5B+ (with the plus sign). |
| A/B tests | 1M+ | >1M | Equivalent. Use "1M+" as the standard formatting. |
| Conversion lift | +43% | varies by vertical | +43% is the cross-vertical average. Use vertical-specific stats when the vertical is known. |
| Years of data | 10+ years | 9 years | Use "10+ years" — this is the current approved figure as of FY27. |
