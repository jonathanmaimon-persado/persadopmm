# Co-Branded Cards (Issuer + Partner)

## Vertical Profile

**Primary pain:** Dual-approval cycles between bank and partner kill campaign timelines. Every asset needs sign-off from both the issuer's compliance team AND the partner's brand team. What's already a 6-8 week cycle becomes 10-12 weeks.

**Secondary pains:**
- Partner brand guidelines layered on top of regulatory requirements
- Seasonal promotions miss launch windows due to dual-review bottleneck
- Creative agencies struggle with dual-brand compliance
- High cost of rework when one side rejects

## Speed Advantage

One brief, dual-compliant output for both brands.

**Specific hooks:**
- "Dual-brand compliance in the generation, not in the review cycle."
- "Your co-brand holiday campaign — ready in days, approved by both brands."
- "One brief. Both brands' guidelines. Production-ready in 72 hours."

## Key Regulations

- **TILA** — APR disclosures, fee disclosures, promotional rate terms
- **UDAAP** — Fair marketing language, no misleading claims
- **Partner Brand Guidelines** — Voice, tone, logo usage, color palette, messaging dos/don'ts
- Dual compliance = bank regulatory frameworks + partner brand standards simultaneously

## The Dual-Compliance Differentiator

This is where Persado's advantage is most acute. Generic AI tools have zero awareness of:
- Partner-specific brand voice requirements
- Co-brand visual and messaging hierarchy rules
- The interaction between regulatory compliance and brand compliance
- Approval workflow requirements for dual-brand content

Persado can be trained on both the issuer's regulatory framework AND the partner's brand guidelines, producing content that satisfies both in a single generation cycle.

## Key Objection to Address

**"Our partner approval process is the bottleneck, not content creation."**

Response: When content arrives pre-validated against both brand and regulatory guidelines, the approval cycle compresses. Compliance review becomes confirmation, not correction. Partners approve faster when there's less to fix.
