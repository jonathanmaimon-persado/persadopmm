# Competitive Landscape

## The "Baby Carrot Strategy"

Persado does not compete on AI features against ChatGPT/Jasper. It competes on **agency budgets** against Publicis/Havas/VML/McCann. Same core technology, different competitive set, different budget, different conversation.

This reframe is critical: when positioned against generic AI tools, Persado is "expensive AI." When positioned against agency retainers, Persado is "75% cheaper with better results."

## Competitor Breakdown

### Traditional Agencies
**Examples:** Publicis, Havas, VML, McCann

**Where they fail:**
- 6-8 week production cycles
- $800K+ annual retainers
- No performance optimization built in
- Can't pivot to AI without cannibalizing their own business model
- Compliance is a manual review layer, not built into creation

**Persado's wedge:**
- 4x faster delivery
- 75% lower cost
- Continuous learning from performance data
- Compliance validated during generation

**Displacement math:** 50 agency assets @ $15K = $750K. Persado: 50 credits x $4.5K = $225K. Save $525K.

### In-House Studios
**Examples:** Chase Inner Circle, Wells Fargo "Wello"

**Where they fail:**
- Can't scale beyond headcount
- Production bottleneck — queue of briefs waiting
- No regulatory specialization in creative tools
- Hiring is slow and expensive

**Persado's wedge:**
- Professional output without additional headcount
- Scales instantly with demand
- Regulatory expertise built in

### Generic AI Writing Tools
**Examples:** Jasper, Copy.ai, ChatGPT

**Where they fail:**
- Produce drafts, not production-ready assets
- Zero compliance awareness
- No performance intelligence — starts from zero every time
- No financial services specialization
- Require human review and rework for regulated content

**Persado's wedge:**
- Production-ready output (not drafts)
- Regulated-industry specialization
- 96% win rate vs. human-written AND generic LLM content
- 10+ years and 1M+ A/B tests in financial services
- Compliance during generation, not after

### Martech / Personalization
**Examples:** Movable Ink, Jacquard

**Where they fail:**
- Requires your team to execute — tool, not solution
- No compliance layer
- Widget-level personalization only (subject lines, hero images)
- Doesn't create full assets

**Persado's wedge:**
- Full-asset creation + compliance + optimization
- End-to-end from brief to production-ready
- API-level personalization for real-time content

### Optimization / Testing Platforms
**Examples:** Optimizely, VWO

**Where they fail:**
- Testing platform, not content platform
- Helps you test variants but doesn't create them
- Doesn't solve the production bottleneck

**Persado's wedge:**
- Creates the variants AND optimizes them
- Performance prediction before launch (scored 0-100)
- Reduces testing cycles by generating winning variants

### Compliance Software
**Examples:** PerformLine, Red Flag

**Where they fail:**
- Reactive scanning — reviews after content is created
- No performance optimization
- No content production capability
- Adds time to the process instead of removing it

**Persado's wedge:**
- Proactive validation during generation
- Performance + compliance scored together
- Content creation + compliance in one step

## Key Competitive Talking Points

1. **vs. Agencies:** "Same quality, 75% less cost, 4x faster. No retainer required."
2. **vs. ChatGPT/Jasper:** "They give you drafts. We give you production-ready assets with compliance built in. 96% win rate."
3. **vs. Movable Ink:** "They personalize widgets. We create, optimize, and personalize complete assets — with compliance."
4. **vs. Optimizely/VWO:** "They test what you already have. We create what performs better."
5. **vs. In-house teams:** "Scale output without scaling headcount."
